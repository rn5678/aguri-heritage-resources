Read this resource for Aguri / Ugra Kshatriya history in English or consult the Bengali original. Bandhu develops an argument connecting the community with Rajput ancestry, drawing on historical texts and inscriptions. His interpretation can be read alongside the other sources gathered on this website.[cite:rajput-1940]

Achintya Kumar Dutta discusses the 1913 edition in his study of community organisation and claims to Kshatriya recognition (2023, p. 315). That reference concerns an earlier edition than the revised 1940 text translated here. Read how the book fits into the wider history of community identity.[cite:dutta-2023]
