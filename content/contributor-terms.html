What to submit

Submit material you created, are authorised to share, or can lawfully supply for the intended use. Give available sources, dates, permissions and context; distinguish remembered accounts from verified facts. Owning a physical photograph or document does not necessarily mean owning its copyright. Obtain any necessary consent before sharing another person’s private information or images. Do not submit unlawful material or knowingly false claims.

Credit, privacy and editorial review

Tell us the public credit you prefer, or request anonymity. Your contact details are used privately for editorial follow-up and managing your submission; they are not included in public credit. Editors may select, defer or decline material. Submission does not guarantee publication, payment or endorsement.

Corrections and withdrawal

You may request a correction, credit change or withdrawal, identifying the contribution and your connection to it. We will make reasonable efforts to remove withdrawn material from future public display under our control. Limited copies may remain in preservation backups or records needed to manage the request. We cannot recall third-party copies or revoke independently valid licences already granted.

Which terms apply

The submission records the version you accept. Later changes do not expand permission for an earlier contribution without your agreement. Earlier submissions remain subject to their existing permissions. Please read the copyright and reuse policy before reusing other people’s material.
