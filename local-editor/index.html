Editing tips

Choose “Add text block” to write a paragraph beside the selected block, or use Add above / Add below. Delete block removes the selected text block, including headings and captions. Delete photo also removes its attached caption. Restore and “Undo block change” bring them back with your unsaved edits before saving. Use Jump to text to move around the page.

Select words for bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, superscript, subscript, links or Clear format. Click within a block to change its alignment. Clear format keeps links and source references; Unlink removes a selected link. A whole block that is already a page link keeps its destination. Normal Undo works for text and formatting edits.

Click a reference’s × to remove that reference, or focus it and press Delete. Saving renumbers references and removes unused notes. Photos can be JPEG, PNG or WebP, up to 10 MB. Save, then Preview to review both languages. Publish via GitHub when the update is ready.
